icarus.vcd: written by Icarus Verilog 13.0 (stable) (v13_0-dirty), vvp
with $dumpvars(0, tb), on Arch Linux (x86-64), 23 Nov 2026.

The design is make/counter.sv (an 8-bit counter under a testbench), built
with DUMPVARS and DUMPOFF defined (make/make.sh has the commands). What it
shows: $timescale over three lines; a $dumpall of a parameter's value
before #0; aliases (the clock, enable, reset and wrap flag seen from the
counter share the testbench's codes); x and z (a nibble 1x0z, a bus that
floats as bz); a 96-bit bus with x and z; a real (r0, r1.5, r-0.00225,
r6.02e+23, rNaN while dumping is off); an integer; an event, written in
$dumpvars, in $dumpon and when it fires; $dumpoff listing every variable
but the parameter as x, and $dumpon listing them again; a last #time with
no change after it.

icarus.fst: the same run written as FST, by the same Icarus Verilog 13.0
with `vvp -n tb-fst.vvp -fst` (its own copy of libfst's writer), on Arch
Linux (x86-64), 23 Nov 2026 (make/make.sh has the commands). What it
shows, beside what icarus.vcd does: the same variables, aliases and
changes, the real declared 8 bytes long; $dumpoff as FST's dump activity,
with the x values Icarus writes there; a hierarchy packed with zlib;
source files and lines as attributes, and the counter's scope with its
module's name.
