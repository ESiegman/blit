ila_one.vcd, ila_one.csv, ila_one-legacy.csv, ila_windows.vcd,
ila_windows.csv: ILA captures written by Vivado v2026.2 (64-bit, SW
Build 6713330, Basic licence) with write_hw_ila_data, from a Digilent
Basys 3 (xc7a35tcpg236-1) on Arch Linux (x86-64), 23 Nov 2026.

The design is make/ila_top.v (a counter on the board's 100 MHz clock, a
flag the cycle after it is 0xfe, a two-bit phase that steps each time it
wraps, a 12-bit sum lagging the counter by one, four switches, all off),
with the ILA IP on five probes of 1024 samples, built in batch mode by
make/ila_build.tcl; make/ila_capture.tcl programmed the FPGA (its
configuration memory, not the board's flash), armed the ILA twice and
wrote each capture (the Tcl has the trigger settings).

ila_one: one window of 1024 samples, triggered where count is 0x40, at
sample 100; sum shown unsigned and sw_q in binary, the rest in hex.
ila_windows: four windows of 256 samples, each triggered where wrap
rises, at sample 32 of each; count shown signed, phase in octal.

What they show: the VCD form's $timescale of 1ps over times that are
sample numbers (0 to 1023), its probes in a scope "dut" with Vivado's own
_TRIGGER, _WINDOW and _GAP beside them (_TRIGGER flips at each trigger,
_WINDOW at each window's first sample); the CSV form's two first lines,
the columns (Sample in Buffer, Sample in Window, TRIGGER, then a probe
each, "count[7:0]") and their radixes ("Radix - UNSIGNED,...,HEX,..."),
then a sample a line with TRIGGER 1 at each trigger; ila_one-legacy.csv,
written with -legacy_csv_file, the same with no radix row.
