verilator.vcd: written by Verilator 5.054 (2026-11-01, rev v5.054) with
--binary --trace, the testbench's $dumpvars(0, tb), on Arch Linux (x86-64),
23 Nov 2026.

The design is make/counter.sv, the same as icarus.vcd's, built with
DUMPVARS and DUMPOFF defined (make/make.sh has the commands). What it
shows: Verilator's indented header with no $dumpvars block; two states
only (4'b1x0z is written 1000, and z as 0); aliases across the hierarchy;
a 96-bit bus; a real declared 64 bits wide; a parameter as a 32-bit wire;
an event that fires once; $dumpoff and $dumpon ignored (nothing written).

verilator.fst: the same run written as FST, by Verilator 5.054 with
--binary --trace-fst (its own C++ writer, libfstwriter, since 5.050), on
Arch Linux (x86-64), 23 Nov 2026 (make/make.sh has the commands). What it
shows, beside what verilator.vcd does: the same changes, under
SystemVerilog's kinds (logic, and the parameter as a parameter, where the
VCD says wire); ranges after a space ("count [7:0]"); directions on the
counter's ports; a hierarchy packed with LZ4; no $dumpoff (as in its VCD).
