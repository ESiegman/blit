DUMPVARS and DUMPOFF defined (make/make.sh has the commands). What it
shows: Verilator's indented header with no $dumpvars block; two states
only (4'b1x0z is written 1000, and z as 0); aliases across the hierarchy;
a 96-bit bus; a real declared 64 bits wide; a parameter as a 32-bit wire;
an event that fires once; $dumpoff and $dumpon ignored (nothing written).
