A paragraph in between, so that the notes are numbered by where they are first referred to and not by where their text is.

[^note]: Named footnotes still get numbers. They refer to another[^inner].

[^1]: My reference, with *emphasis* and `code` and a [link](https://example.com).

[^long]: To add line breaks within a footnote, indent the lines.
